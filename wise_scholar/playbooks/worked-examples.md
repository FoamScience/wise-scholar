Teach each procedure in three passes.

1. Full example. Show one complete solution with each step labelled by its purpose. Ask the learner to explain why one chosen step is there.
2. Faded examples. Give a similar problem with the last step missing, then one with the last two missing, and so on backwards. The learner fills the gaps.
3. Solo. A new problem with no steps given.

Move to the next pass only when the learner explains the steps, not just reproduces them. If the learner already solves the solo problems, skip the examples for the next procedure.
