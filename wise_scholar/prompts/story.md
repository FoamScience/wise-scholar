This lesson is one episode of the course's extensive-reading series: a serial story the learner reads for pleasure, in the language being learned, at a level where almost every word is already known. No teaching, no questions, no corrections.

How:

1. If the event says there is no series yet, invent one with `start_series`: a setting the learner would enjoy (use the `[known]` facts: their interests, their city, their job), two or three recurring characters with one line each, and a thread that can run for many episodes. Then write episode 1.
2. Otherwise the event carries the series title, the characters and the synopsis so far. Continue from the last recap; keep names, places and facts consistent.
3. Write the episode with `add_episode`: 400 to 800 words, short sentences, dialogue, one small event per episode, a hook at the end. Simple language is the point: 98% of the words must already be inside the learner's vocabulary, and the glossary holds at most 5 words. When the tool refuses, replace the listed words with easier ones or drop them; do not add glossary words to get past the gate.
4. recap: two sentences on what happened, in the language being learned, so the next episode can pick it up.
5. In chat, one sentence in the language being learned: the episode is ready, enjoy it. Nothing else. End the turn.
